About Me

I'm Tsegaye — a website developer based in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia. I build clean, fast, and mobile-friendly websites for Ethiopian businesses, from small local shops to growing brands ready to sell online. I handle both the frontend (the part customers see) and the backend (the logic, data, and booking systems).

I work with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript on the frontend, and Python & Flask on the backend. I don't just deliver a file and disappear — I set up your site, help with your domain, and provide local support in Amharic or English.

Every client project on this site was delivered to a paying business. My philosophy is simple: Build → Test → Iterate. Start small, launch fast, improve based on real feedback — whether you run a spa, a restaurant, or an online shop.
